# 📊 Business Performance Dashboard
### End-to-End Invoice Processing & Analytics

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze the complete invoice processing lifecycle — from invoice receipt and creation to posting and payment.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![Business Performance Dashboard](Business%20Performance%20Dashboard%20(%20End%20to%20End%20Invoice%20Processing%20And%20Analytics).png)

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to track how invoices move through the processing cycle and identify delays, exceptions, payment patterns and workflow backlogs using an interactive Excel dashboard.

## 📂 Dataset

- 500 invoice records
- January–December 2020
- 4 regions

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Invoice processing cycle-time analysis
- Invoice volume by user
- Invoice distribution by region
- Monthly spending trend
- Days Payable Outstanding (DPO)
- Electronic vs manual payment analysis
- Monthly invoice volume
- Invoice status analysis
- Region, user and month slicers
- KPI cards for management reporting

---

## 🔎 Key Findings

### Payment is the slowest stage

Posted → Paid averages 6.07 days, representing approximately 60% of the overall 10.06-day end-to-end cycle.

### High exception rate

49.4% of invoices were flagged as exceptions.

### Workflow backlog

212 invoices were still in workflow, with 111 waiting at the Received → Created stage and 101 at Created → Posted.

### Payment mix

Among the 288 paid invoices:

- ACH: 149 invoices (52%)
- Check: 139 invoices (48%)

### Monthly volume

October recorded the highest invoice volume with 51 invoices, followed by March with 49 invoices.

---

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis indicates that payment processing is the largest contributor to overall invoice cycle time.

The workflow backlog is concentrated in the early stages of invoice processing, particularly the Received → Created stage.

The high exception rate also indicates an opportunity to review invoice validation and exception-handling processes.
